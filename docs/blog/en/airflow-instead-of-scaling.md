# Air polishing or scaling? What to expect at a dental cleaning

AIRFLOW air polishing removes soft plaque and surface stains. Hardened deposits, called tartar or calculus, may need ultrasonic or hand scaling.

## Why both tools may be used at the same visit

Air polishing uses a stream of water, air and fine powder. It can help remove the soft bacterial film and surface staining you may notice between visits. A scaler is used where deposits have hardened. If you have signs of gum disease, the clinician may recommend a separate assessment and treatment plan.

## Tell your clinician what has made cleaning difficult before

Mention sensitive teeth, bleeding gums or discomfort during a previous cleaning. Ask what the clinician expects to use and whether anything can be adjusted for you. The timing of your next visit depends on how deposits build up and how healthy your gums are.
