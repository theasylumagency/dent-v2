# Digital dental impressions: when a TRIOS scan can be used

A TRIOS intraoral scanner captures the surfaces of your teeth and visible tissues. The resulting 3D model can be used to plan a crown, bridge or other restoration.

## What happens during the scan?

The dentist moves a small scanner around your mouth while the model appears on a screen. If a section needs more detail, that part can often be scanned again. The dentist can send the digital model to the dental laboratory. Scan time depends on the area and the amount of detail the case needs.

## Will I still need an impression tray?

For some full-arch or removable restorations, the dentist may choose a conventional impression or use both techniques. What matters is capturing the information the laboratory needs to make a restoration that fits. If impression trays have been difficult for you in the past, mention this when you discuss the plan.
