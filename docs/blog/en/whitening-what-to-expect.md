# What does “eight shades whiter” actually mean?

“Eight shades” refers to steps on a particular shade guide. Your starting colour, the source of discolouration and the whitening method all affect the result.

## Ask how your result will be measured

Ask your dentist to record your starting shade and explain how they will compare it with the result. If the colour you notice comes mainly from surface staining, professional cleaning may be part of the discussion. Whitening gel acts on the colour of natural teeth.

## Plan around visible fillings, crowns and veneers

Whitening can lighten the surrounding teeth while fillings, crowns and veneers keep their colour. If you have restorations at the front, talk about the expected colour match before treatment. Mention existing sensitivity, too. Temporary sensitivity and gum irritation can occur after whitening.
